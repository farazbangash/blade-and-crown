Our Story

About Blade & Crown

Founded in 2019 in the heart of Chicago's River North district, Blade & Crown was built on one belief — every man deserves a barbershop experience that matches his standard.

From classic cuts to modern skin fades, hot towel shaves to our signature Crown VIP experience, every service is delivered with precision, care, and respect for the craft. Our barbers don't just cut hair — they build confidence.
